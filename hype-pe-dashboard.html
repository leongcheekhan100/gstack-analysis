Market cap vs annualized earnings (log)

When the two lines diverge, the P/E expands or compresses.

Methodology. Market cap = CoinGecko's reported daily market cap. Earnings = daily USD value of HYPE bought back & burned by the Assistance Fund, sourced from DeFiLlama's "holders revenue" series for hyperliquid-perps. P/E = market cap ÷ (earnings × 365). Crypto P/E is an annualized-yield convention, not a GAAP earnings ratio: it answers "how many years of current buybacks does the market cap represent?".
